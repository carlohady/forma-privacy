2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Forma handles personal information when you connect your WHOOP account. WHOOP separately processes information under its own privacy policy. Forma is not owned by, endorsed by, or affiliated with WHOOP, Inc.

3. Information requested from WHOOP

Forma will request access only after you choose to connect WHOOP and approve the OAuth consent screen. The WHOOP API can make the following categories available, depending on the scopes Forma requests:

  • basic profile information, such as name, email address, and WHOOP user ID;
  • recovery information, including recovery score, heart-rate variability, and resting heart rate;
  • physiological cycle and strain information;
  • workout information, including activity type, strain, heart-rate summaries, and timestamps;
  • sleep information, including sleep performance, duration, and sleep-stage summaries;
  • body measurements, such as height, weight, and maximum heart rate; and
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection.
